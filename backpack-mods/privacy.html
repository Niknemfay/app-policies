Privacy Policy

App: Backpack Mods for MCPE (com.backpackmods.addons.mcpe)
Developer: Codeum Games
Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information the app processes and why. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number or precise location.

1. Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob and its mediation partners (for example Unity Ads, AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Liftoff and ironSource). To show and measure ads, these services may collect the Android Advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, language and information about ad interactions. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings inside the app. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your Android settings.

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Unity privacy policy

2. Analytics and app configuration

We use Unity Gaming Services (Unity Analytics and Remote Config) to understand how the app is used (for example which screens are opened, which mods are installed, ad and purchase events) and to change app settings without an update. Unity assigns an anonymous player ID. Analytics data is collected only after you give consent where consent is required.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation of which products you own so we can unlock them.

4. Notifications

The app can show local reminders about new mods. They are created on your device and can be turned off in Android settings.

5. Files

When you install a mod, the app copies the addon file into its own storage and passes it to Minecraft, or saves it to your Downloads folder if you choose so. The app does not read other files on your device.

6. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Data retention and deletion

Settings and favorites are stored on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the app. To request deletion of analytics data linked to your anonymous ID, contact us at the address below.
